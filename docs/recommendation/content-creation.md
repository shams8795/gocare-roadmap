# Content Creation

## 1. Feature Overview

### The problem

Every product, campaign, and page on GoCare needs written content. Today, this content is written manually by the team or by sellers. As a result:

- Some products have very short or missing descriptions.
- Product descriptions are written in different styles and quality levels.
- Writing marketing content in Arabic and English for thousands of products takes a lot of time.
- Pages are not always written in a way that helps them appear in search engines such as Google.

### What the feature does

**Content Creation** uses AI to generate content automatically. The roadmap describes three parts:

- **AI-generated product descriptions:** writing a clear description for each product, in Arabic and English, based on its name, attributes, and images.
- **SEO-optimized marketing copy:** writing product pages and marketing texts in a way that helps them rank higher in search engines.
- **Personalized content feeds:** showing each user content that matches their interests, such as tips, articles, and products.

**Example:**

| Case                | Before                               | After                                                                                                  |
|---------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Product description | "Cream 50 ml"                        | A full description explaining the product type, suitable skin type, key ingredients, and how to use it |
| Search visibility   | Product page with a short title only | A page with a clear title and description that matches what users search for on Google                 |
| Homepage            | The same content for every user      | A user interested in hair care sees hair care tips and products first                                  |

In short, the feature turns limited product information into complete, consistent, and personalized content.

## 2. Current Website Status

Based on the review in this report:

- GoCare offers many products from different brands and sellers.
- The website supports Arabic and English.
- Product descriptions vary in length, style, and quality.
- The AI Agent team already has work related to **content enhancement** in the chatbot.

**What does this mean for the feature?**

- There is a clear need for consistent, high-quality product content.
- The existing content enhancement work in the AI Agent team may be reused, so the work must be aligned to avoid building the same thing twice.

## 3. Business Objective

The main goal is to improve product content quality and visibility, which helps users make buying decisions and brings more visitors from search engines.

**Expected benefits:**

- **Complete product pages:** every product has a clear and useful description.
- **More visitors from search engines:** better SEO brings more free traffic.
- **Faster product onboarding:** new products are ready to sell faster.
- **More engagement:** personalized content keeps users interested.

**Example:**

A new product added by a seller without a description gets a complete Arabic and English description in minutes, instead of waiting for the team to write it.

**Priority:** Long-term vision, according to the roadmap.

## 4. Functional Requirements

**FR-01: Product description generation**

The system must generate product descriptions in Arabic and English from product names, attributes, and images.

**FR-02: SEO-optimized content**

The system must generate titles and descriptions that include relevant search keywords.

**FR-03: Personalized content feeds**

The system must show each user content that matches their interests.

**FR-04: Human review**

Generated content must be reviewable and editable before it is published.

**Proposed additional requirements (need client confirmation):**

- **FR-05:** Generating marketing copy for campaigns, connected to Personalized Marketing.
- **FR-06:** Checking generated content against a list of forbidden claims.
- **FR-07:** Measuring the impact of new content on visits and sales.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

**1. Accuracy**

Generated content must not include wrong information about products.

**2. Safety & Compliance**

Content must avoid medical claims and follow advertising rules in Saudi Arabia.

**3. Consistency**

Content must follow GoCare's brand voice in both languages.

**4. Language Quality**

Arabic content must be natural and correct, not a literal translation from English.

**5. Traceability**

It must be clear which content was generated by AI and who approved it.

## 7. Technical Feasibility

Large Language Models (LLMs) are very capable of writing product descriptions and marketing texts in Arabic and English. The first version can:

- Use an LLM with a fixed template and product attributes as input.
- Apply a brand style guide and a list of forbidden claims.
- Send generated content for human review before publishing.

**Initial assessment:** technically feasible with current models. The main effort is in quality control, brand voice, and avoiding wrong or unsafe claims.

## 8. Dependencies & Constraints

### Dependencies

- Good product attributes, supported by Automated Content Tagging.
- A brand style guide and content rules.
- Alignment with the AI Agent team's content enhancement work.
- A review process with the content or marketing team.

### Constraints & Risks

**1. Wrong information**

AI may invent product details that are not true.

**2. Medical claims**

Skin care content may include claims such as "treats acne", which may not be allowed.

**3. Repetitive content**

Generated texts may sound too similar, which can hurt SEO and user experience.

**4. Arabic quality**

Generated Arabic may sound unnatural without careful prompts and review.

**Example:**

If the AI writes "this cream treats eczema" for a normal moisturizer, it creates a false medical claim, which is why human review and a forbidden claims list are required.

## Meeting Decisions

The following points were agreed in the meeting:

**1. The feature already exists**

The team already has an AI content creation feature for product descriptions.

**2. Current problems**

The existing feature has several problems:

- **Edits are rejected:** if the team edits anything in the description generated by the AI, the system rejects the edit, so they cannot change the text.
- **Required information that is not needed:** the system forces the team to fill in specific product information, such as the expiry date or the country of origin, before it generates a description.
- **Unimportant data:** this information is not important to the team, and filling it in for every product takes extra time.

**3. What the team needs**

- **Editable descriptions:** the team must be able to edit the AI-generated description freely, and the edits must be saved.
- **Only important fields:** the system should provide fields only for the information that is important for the product, and the team fills in these fields only.
- **No forced fields:** information that is not important should be optional, or removed, instead of being required.
